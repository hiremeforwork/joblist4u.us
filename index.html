Submit a Job Posting

Employers may submit employment opportunities to: submission@joblist4u.us

Review Process

  • All submissions are manually reviewed before publication.
  • JobList4U.us reserves the right to reject or remove postings.
  • Fraudulent, misleading, discriminatory, or unlawful postings are prohibited.
  • Jobs must comply with applicable federal, state, and local employment laws.
  • Positions may be removed upon expiration or employer request.

Required Submission Information

  • Employer legal name
  • Employer website
  • Business address
  • Hiring contact name
  • Contact email or application URL
  • Job title
  • Job description
  • Location
  • Employment type
  • Compensation details
  • Application deadline
  • Start date

Compliance Requirements

  • Compliance with FLSA wage and hour requirements.
  • No deceptive earnings claims.
  • Compensation disclosure where required by state law.
  • Equal Employment Opportunity compliance.
  • No discriminatory hiring practices.
  • No pyramid schemes or multi-level marketing opportunities.
  • No positions violating federal or state law.
  • Independent contractor positions must be clearly identified.
  • Remote positions must identify work authorization requirements.
